# Paperpath Support

Paperpath shows the paperwork steps after a move, what each step needs, and what it gives
you.

## Common questions

**Where does the starter list come from?**
It lists the steps most students from outside the EU go through in Germany. Rules differ
by city, so check your office's website. You can change or delete every step.

**How do I change the move-in date?**
On the Steps tab, tap Move-In Date at the top. Deadlines that count from it update.

**I marked a step as done by mistake.**
Open the step and tap Mark as Not Done. Documents you received stay in your folder; turn
them off on the Documents tab if needed.

**Is my data backed up?**
It is part of your normal iPhone backup. Paperpath itself sends nothing anywhere.

**How do I delete everything?**
Delete the app from your iPhone.
