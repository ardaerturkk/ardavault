# Halfday Support

Halfday counts the working days a student from outside the EU uses in Germany: each
shift becomes a full or half day, and the app shows how many days are left this year and
when a planned schedule would reach the limit.

## Common questions

**Which rules does Halfday use?**
Typical rules as of 2026: more than 4 hours on a date is a full day, up to 4 hours a half
day, 140 full or 280 half days per calendar year. Student assistant and tutor jobs at the
university can be marked as not counted. Your residence permit, its supplementary sheet
and your Ausländerbehörde decide what applies to you. Halfday is not legal advice.

**The rules changed. Can I adjust them?**
Yes. Open Settings and tap any limit: full days per year, the half-day threshold, hours
per week and the Minijob limit. Restore Typical Values brings back the defaults.

**Two shifts on one day: how do they count?**
Halfday adds up the hours of all counted jobs on the same date. Two 3-hour shifts make
one full day.

**Do vacation or sick days count?**
Usually not. Simply do not log them.

**My office counts 2.5 days per week instead. Can Halfday do that?**
Not in this version. The How Halfday Counts page in Settings explains this method; ask
your office which one applies to you.

**Is my data backed up?**
It is part of your normal iPhone backup. Halfday itself sends nothing anywhere.

**How do I delete everything?**
Delete the app from your iPhone.
