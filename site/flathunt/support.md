# Flatboard Support

Flatboard keeps every flat you are chasing on one board: where you found it, where you
stand, when you view it, what it really costs, and a short safety checklist.

## Common questions

**What is warm rent?**
The rent including heating and running costs (Nebenkosten). Flatboard compares flats by
warm rent, per month or per square metre, because cold rent alone hides the real cost.

**Why is a flat missing on the Compare tab?**
Compare needs the warm rent, and the size for the per m² view. Such flats are listed
under Not Compared; open one and tap Edit to add the numbers. Declined flats are left out.

**How do I open a listing?**
Open the flat and tap Copy Link, then paste it into your browser. Flatboard itself makes
no network connections.

**Is the safety checklist legal advice?**
No. It lists general precautions against common rental scams. When in doubt, ask the
Studentenwerk or a local tenants' association (Mieterverein) before you pay or send
documents.

**I moved a flat to the wrong stage.**
Open it and tap Stage to choose any stage.

**How do I delete everything?**
Delete the app from your iPhone.
