# Sagbar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Sagbar does not collect any data.

- There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- The app makes no network requests. It works fully offline.
- The personal details you enter (such as your name, date of birth, address, phone number,
  email, health insurer, insurance number, student number, appointment times and
  reference numbers) and the lines you write are stored only on your iPhone, in the
  app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere.
- They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) the same way as other
  app data, under your control.
- Nobody, including the developer, can see what you enter.
- When you copy a line, it goes to your iPhone's clipboard, like any text you copy.

## Deleting your data

To remove a single detail, open My Details, tap it, clear the text and tap Save. To delete
your own lines, touch and hold a line and choose Delete Line. To remove everything, delete
the app from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.
