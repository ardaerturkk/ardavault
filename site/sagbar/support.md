# Sagbar Support

Sagbar prepares short, polite German lines for offices and phone calls, with your own
details already filled in, and shows them in large type at the counter.

## Common questions

**Why does a line show something like [Date of Birth]?**
That detail is still missing. Open the situation: missing details are listed at the top
under Fill In Missing Details. Tap one to add it.

**Where do I enter my Aktenzeichen or appointment time?**
Open the situation (for example Ausländerbehörde). Under This Visit, tap Appointment or
the number field. Each situation keeps its own values.

**Can I change a built-in line?**
Built-in lines cannot be edited, but you can hide them (touch and hold, then Hide Line)
and add your own line with the + button. Hidden lines come back with Show Hidden Lines.

**Why is the meaning in English when my iPhone is set to German?**
Meanings are shown in English or Turkish. With a German interface, the English meaning
is shown.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. The lines are everyday polite German for common moments. Each office has its own
rules; check its website or letter.

**Is my data backed up?**
It is part of your normal iPhone backup. Sagbar itself sends nothing anywhere.

**How do I delete everything?**
Delete the app from your iPhone.
