# Idea: Paperpath (working name)

Date: 2026-09-23. Stage: passed Discover.

## Pain (Arda, user number one)
Arda moves from Turkey to Kiel for a master's. The first weeks are a chain of German
paperwork where each office wants a document that another office hands out:
Wohnungsgeberbestätigung -> Anmeldung -> Meldebescheinigung -> bank account, tax ID,
Rundfunkbeitrag, residence permit. People lose days showing up without the one paper
they needed, or miss the 14-day Anmeldung window.

## Competitors (App Store / web, searched 2026-09-23)
- Apple Reminders / any to-do app: flat lists, no idea that one step unlocks another or
  what to bring.
- Document expiry trackers (Document Expiry Reminder, RemindMe, KeepValid, RenewalKit):
  track when a card expires, not how you get it or what it is needed for. Saturated.
- Relocation guides (VisaFlow, SettlyGo, expat blogs): web content or accounts, not a
  personal, offline, editable plan.

## Wedge (one sentence)
Unlike Reminders or an expiry tracker, Paperpath knows which documents each step needs
and which document it gives you, so it always shows what you can do today and exactly
what to bring, offline and without an account.

## Why it can be great
Small data model (documents + steps with needs/produces), one strong moment (marking a
step done hands you a new document and unlocks the next steps), a useful starter
template for students moving to Germany, and everything editable for any country.

## Risks
- Rules differ by city: the template is editable and says so; no legal claims.
- Name: "Papierkram" is taken (Papierkram.de); "Paperpath" is a working name.
