# Idea: Flat hunt tracker (working name "Flatboard")

Date: 2026-09-23. Stage: Discover, candidate. Blocked on one question to Arda (is he
still looking for a room/flat in Kiel?). If not, user number one has no pain: park it.

## Pain
Students in German university towns write dozens of messages across WG-Gesucht,
Kleinanzeigen, ImmoScout24, Facebook groups and the Studentenwerk. Who answered, which
viewing is when, which flat asked for which papers (Schufa, income proof,
Mietschuldenfreiheitsbescheinigung), and which offer smelled like a scam: all of it is
lost in chat apps and bookmarks.

## Competitors (searched 2026-09-23)
- Portal apps (ImmoScout24, immowelt, HOWOGE): favorites and appointments only for their
  own listings.
- Notes / Reminders / spreadsheets: no pipeline, no viewing view, no cost comparison.

## Wedge (one sentence)
One offline pipeline for every flat from every portal (Interested, Messaged, Viewing,
Applied, Answer) with viewing times, warm-rent comparison per month and per square
metre, and a short scam checklist, which no portal can offer because each sees only its
own listings.

## Risks
- Short life per user (weeks), so retention does not matter but App Store discovery does.
- Scam checklist is content that must stay careful and generic.
- Overlaps Paperpath's "documents" idea; keep it out (link to Paperpath instead).
