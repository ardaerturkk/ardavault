Flatboard keeps your flat hunt in one place. Rooms and flats come from listing sites, group chats, the student housing office and friends. Flatboard puts every one of them on a single board and shows where you stand with each.

Made for students looking for a room in a German university town, and useful for any rental search.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Add a flat in seconds: title, where you found it, the listing link, warm and cold rent, size, district and notes. Only the title is required.
- Move each flat along: Interested, Messaged, Viewing, Applied, then Accepted or Declined. One button always takes it to the next step.
- Keep viewing times. Upcoming viewings stand out in the list, past ones step back.
- Compare flats by warm rent per month or per square metre, cheapest first, and see how much more each one costs.
- Go through a short safety checklist for every flat: seen in person, no money before a viewing and a signed contract, no transfers abroad or through cash transfer services, ID copies only when the contract is ready, rent that fits the area.
- Copy a saved link to open it in your browser.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your iPhone and the app works fully offline.

The safety checklist lists general precautions. It is not legal advice; when in doubt, ask your student services or a local tenants' association.

Available in English, German and Turkish.
