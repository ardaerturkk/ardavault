Flatboard works fully offline and needs no account or login. To see the main flow: on the empty Flats screen tap Add Flat, type a title and a warm rent (for example 450) and a size (for example 18), then tap Save. Open the flat and tap Mark as Messaged, then Schedule Viewing, pick a time and tap Done. The Compare tab lists flats by warm rent per month or per square metre. Each flat has a Safety Checks list of general precautions against rental scams. Links are stored as text only and can be copied; the app opens no web pages, collects no data and makes no network requests.
