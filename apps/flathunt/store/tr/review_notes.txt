Flatboard is fully offline and needs no account. Main flow: on the empty Evler screen tap Ev Ekle, enter a title, a warm rent (for example 450) and a size (for example 18), tap Kaydet. Open the flat, tap Mesaj Attım, then Ev Gezme Randevusu Ekle, pick a time and tap Bitti. The Karşılaştır tab sorts flats by warm rent per month or per square metre. Links are stored as text only and can be copied. The app collects no data and makes no network requests.
