Halfday works fully offline and needs no account or login. To see the main flow: on the Overview tab tap Log Shift, tap Add a Job, enter any name and tap Save, then Save the shift. The number of days left drops from 140 to 139.5 (a 4-hour shift is a half day). Open the Plan tab to see the date a planned schedule would reach the yearly limit. The app collects no data and makes no network requests. It is a counting tool for students in Germany, not legal advice.
