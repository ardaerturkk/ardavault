Sagbar prepares what to say at German offices and on the phone. Save your details once, pick a situation, and get short, polite German lines with your name, address, date of birth and reference numbers already filled in. Under each line you see what it means in English or Turkish.

At the counter, tap a line and it fills the screen in large type, so you can read it out or simply hold up your iPhone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Keep your details in one place: full name, date of birth, address, phone, email, health insurer, insurance number and student number. Every field is optional.
- Pick from eight situations: Bürgeramt (registering your address), Ausländerbehörde (residence permit), bank, Krankenkasse, doctor's reception, calling your landlord, the student office, and basics for any counter, like asking someone to speak more slowly.
- Add the appointment time and the number from your letter (Aktenzeichen, Vorgangsnummer, Kundennummer). They appear in the lines where they belong.
- Show any line as a large card. Swipe to the next one. Copy a line into a message or email.
- Spell your name letter by letter on the phone.
- Add your own lines and hide the ones you do not need.

WHAT IT IS NOT
Sagbar is not a translator and not legal advice. The lines are everyday, polite Standard German for common moments. Each office has its own rules.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your details are stored only on your iPhone and the app works fully offline.

Available in English, German and Turkish. The German lines stay the same in every language.
