Sagbar works fully offline and needs no account or login. To see the main flow: on the Situations tab tap Add My Details, tap Full Name, type any name and tap Save. Back on Situations, open Allgemein: the line "Mein Name ist ..." now contains the name and its spelling. Open Bürgeramt, tap Show Cards to see a line in large type, and use the arrows to move between lines. Touch and hold a line to copy or hide it. The app collects no data and makes no network requests.
